About Us

Who we are

Ashva Engineering Consultancy is a boutique consultancy firm led by Engineer Rajesh, established to guide clients through the strategic, technical, and commercial decisions involved in creating high-quality spaces.

We work at the intersection of vision, design intent, and execution — helping clients translate ideas into well-structured, deliverable outcomes. Our role is to bring clarity at the earliest stages of a project and continuity through its lifecycle, ensuring that design ambition, functionality, timelines, and budgets remain aligned.

Process Overview

From concept to reality

A structured four-stage engagement that keeps ambition, timelines and budgets aligned from the first sketch to handover.

  1. 01

    Conceptual Design

    Generating ideas and innovative solutions. Focused on function and aesthetics — establishing the groundwork for detailed design.

  2. 02

    Detailed Planning

    Turning abstract ideas into actionable plans: components, schematics, material selection and cross-discipline coordination.

  3. 03

    BOQ & Budgeting

    A meticulous breakdown of materials, labor and technical specifications. Transparent pricing that mitigates financial risk.

  4. 04

    Execution & Supervision

    Implementing plans, monitoring progress, managing resources and resolving issues — delivered within timeline and budget.

  • Comprehensive Design. Planning and design for industrial facilities, warehouses, factories and logistics hubs — optimized for storage, production flow, ventilation and fire safety.
